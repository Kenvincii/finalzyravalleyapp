Learn here. Launch here.

As Zyra grows, we will teach more students and hire more people, in Ghana first and then across Africa.

Three ways in.

Education

We will visit high schools across Ghana to run workshops and talks on rockets, robotics, and coding, then continue with colleges and universities. The goal is to teach the skills our own teams use, so a student can go from a first lesson to real work.

Internships

Hands-on placements alongside our engineers on real projects, in aerospace, robotics, research, and motors. Students learn by building things that matter.

Careers

Full-time roles across every division as Zyra scales. We want to hire people from Ghana and across Africa and give them the chance to grow with the company.

Roadmap.

Where Zyra is going, one step at a time. This is our current plan, and it will change as we learn and grow.

Now

Launch the Zyra brand and website, build our community on X, and plan our outreach to high schools across Ghana.

Next

Visit high schools with workshops and talks, extend to colleges and universities, and start our first internships.

Later

Grow our research lab with university partners and build our first robotics and vehicle prototypes.

Aerospace test programs and launch operations in Ghana, with our wormhole research running alongside.

Questions.

Quick answers about Zyra.

What is Zyra?

Zyra is a company being built in Ghana. It is working toward aerospace, robotics, research, and motors, and aims to teach the next generation of African engineers.

Where is Zyra based?

Our headquarters is in Ghana. We are starting here and will bring in people from across Africa and the world.

Is Zyra already building rockets and cars?

Zyra is at an early stage. The pages on this site describe what we are working toward.

Who can join?

Students, engineers, researchers, and partners. Visit the Join page to get in touch.

Is Zyra connected to any school or university?

No. The photos and videos of campuses on this site show places we plan to visit. Zyra is not affiliated with or endorsed by those institutions.
